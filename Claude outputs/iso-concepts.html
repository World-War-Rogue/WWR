World War Rogue

Isometric rebuild

Same four ideas, rebuilt with real geometry: every structure is a box with a lit top, a mid-tone left face and a shaded right face, casting a shadow onto the ground and drawn back-to-front so nearer buildings overlap the ones behind. Compare the small versions to the flat ones — height survives the zoom-out, flat colour does not.

Still drawn from recipes rather than image files, so this scales to any size and needs nothing downloaded. If you want painted illustration rather than clean stylised geometry, that is a commission — these slots would take the art unchanged.
