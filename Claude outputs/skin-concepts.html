World War Rogue

Base skin concepts

Ten silhouettes to choose from. Each is drawn twice: at close-up size, and at the size it actually appears on the world map when you are looking at your neighbours.

The small version is the one that matters. A skin earns its place by being recognisable at map scale — if two bases are indistinguishable at 30 pixels, they are the same skin as far as a player is concerned, however different the detail is up close.

These are drawn from recipes rather than image files: the same code renders them at any size, nothing has to be downloaded, and a skin is a data entry rather than an art pipeline. When you commission real art it drops into these same slots.
