## 3. Strict 5-Squad Limit & 6 Units Per Squad

To ensure deep tactical play instead of chaotic unit swarming, each Commander commands exactly 5 distinct battlegroup squads:

- **SQUAD 1: ALPHA (Spearhead Armored Assault)**  
  Specialization: Heavy MBTs and breaching vehicles designed to crack enemy frontlines.
- **SQUAD 2: BRAVO (Heavy Siege & Artillery Fire)**  
  Specialization: Self-propelled howitzers, MLRS rocket artillery, and demolition engineer vehicles.
- **SQUAD 3: CHARLIE (Reconnaissance & Rapid Interdiction)**  
  Specialization: Fast wheeled AFVs, scout ATVs, sniper observers, and light ATGM platforms.
- **SQUAD 4: DELTA (Air Defense & Electronic Warfare Grid)**  
  Specialization: Mobile SAM launchers, SPAAG radar guns, and anti-drone electronic jamming trucks.
- **SQUAD 5: ECHO (Special Operations & Aerial Strike)**  
  Specialization: Attack helicopters, close air support drones, and elite stealth skirmishers.

### Capacity Rule:
- Each squad holds a maximum of **6 units** (30 deployed units in total combat readiness).
- Every unit has both an explicit **Tactical Advantage** (e.g., *Kinetic ERA stops first HEAT round*) and a **Tactical Vulnerability** (e.g., *25% slower road speed, susceptible to tandem top-attack*).
