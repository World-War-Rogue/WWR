## 1. Five Dynamic Strategic Resources

The forward operating base relies on five interconnected strategic resources:

| Resource | Primary Production | Primary Consumption | Tactical Consequence of Depletion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fuel** | Refineries, Pipeline nodes, Tanker drops | Squad deployments, Radar sweeps, Generators | Vehicles slowed by 50%; air sorties unavailable. |
| **Rations** | Hydroponic farms, Field supply airdrops | Unit maintenance, Recruitment morale | Troop fatigue causes -25% reload and accuracy debuff. |
| **Munitions** | Ammo factories, Munition depots, Rail lines | Ballistic combat, Howitzers, CIWS auto-fire | Defensive turrets cease fire; squads fall back to sidearms. |
| **Alloy** | Heavy smelting plants, Scrap reclamation | Fortification construction, Armor repair | Blast walls crumble without repair; base integrity falls. |
| **War Bonds** | Seasonal milestones, Tactical PvE commendations | Fair Armory supply crates, Cosmetic camos | Slowed acquisition of specialized experimental blueprints. |

## 2. Survival Crisis Events & Night Swarms

Commanders face dynamic survival threats that disrupt standard base routines:
- **Nighttime Insurgent Swarms:** Autonomous AI raiders probing defensive blindspots.
- **Supply Line Interdictions:** Random convoy ambushes that force commanders to deploy Charlie recon squads to secure fuel routes.
- **Resource Depletion Emergencies:** Extreme environmental events (Sandstorms, Blizzards) spiking fuel consumption, requiring emergency ration rationing.

## 3. Anti-Predatory Fair Monetization Philosophy

Traditional mobile strategy titles (such as *Last War*) employ predatory $99-$499 microtransaction spirals, predatory gacha walls, and artificial timers designed to extract high sums from players.

**World War Rogue establishes a player-first economic model:**
1. **$0.99 to $4.99 Micro-Investments:**
   All optional crate purchases in the Fair Armory are capped at accessible price points ($0.99 Tactical Emergency Pack, $2.49 Armored Vanguard Crate, $4.99 Officer High-Command Chest).
2. **Guaranteed Value & No Gacha Traps:**
   Every supply crate displays exact reward tables with zero zero-percent deception.
3. **Daily Free Tactical Air Drops:**
   Every commander receives daily free military supply drops containing high-grade rations, fuel reserves, and munitions.
4. **Skill-Based Counterplay (F2P Viability):**
   A free-to-play commander who masters ballistic cover, armor ricochet angles, and rock-paper-scissors unit counters (e.g. Javelin ATGM ambush against a high-tier heavy tank) can consistently defeat a spending commander with superior positioning.
