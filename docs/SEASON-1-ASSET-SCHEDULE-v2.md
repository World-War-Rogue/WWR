Week 8 — Adds a rear overwatch aircraft, a lighter rotary screen, a lower-armour breach choice, and recon/precision-drone alternatives.

Week 9 — Adds sustainment lift, long-range rotary strike, another fixed-wing screen, and slower/cheaper formation options rather than stronger replacements.

Week 10 — Adds final Season 1 formation choices: a low-cost armour screen, additional strike/screen aircraft profiles, artillery mobility, and compact reconnaissance.
