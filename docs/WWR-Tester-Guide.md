# World War Rogue — Tester Guide

Thanks for testing **World War Rogue** before launch. It runs in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like a real app (full screen, its own icon). Please read the install steps for your phone, then the "What to try" and "How to report a problem" sections.

**Test link:** https://wwr-test.shy-mouse-f4e2.workers.dev

> This is a **test build**. Your account, bases, and progress here are for testing and may be wiped between updates. Don't spend real money — nothing here is a real purchase.

---

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open **Safari** (it has to be Safari, not Chrome, for install to work on iPhone).
2. Go to **https://wwr-test.shy-mouse-f4e2.workers.dev**
3. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
4. Scroll down the share menu and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
6. Close Safari and open **World War Rogue** from the new icon on your home screen. It now runs full screen like an app.

---

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Go to **https://wwr-test.shy-mouse-f4e2.workers.dev**
3. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots, top-right).
4. Tap **Add to Home screen** (on some phones it says **Install app**).
5. Tap **Add** / **Install**, then **Add automatically** if it asks.
6. Open **World War Rogue** from the new icon in your app drawer or home screen.

## Signing in

- On first open you'll be asked to create a commander: pick a **callsign** and a **password**.
- Write your callsign and password down somewhere — there's no email recovery on the test build.
- If you get bumped out, just open the app again and sign back in with the same callsign and password.

## What to try

Spend 10–15 minutes doing normal things, then poke at the newer features. A rough tour:

**The basics**
- Move around the world map (drag to pan, pinch to zoom).
- Open your base and upgrade a building or two.
- Open your Task Forces, drag assets between slots, and upgrade an asset's rank.
- Send a Task Force out on the map and watch it march.

**Daily play** (Tactical Operations Center → Events)
- **Daily Operations**: complete a few lanes and claim the Cache.
- **Map exercises**: find the daily targets near your base and take one.
- **Iron Dominion Arena**: set up your Arena Squad, enter, and watch the battle replay (try Pause, 2×, and Skip to Result). Then open the report, and re-open it later from Reports → Arena.

**Alliances** (needs an alliance)
- Join or create an alliance.
- **Dominion Warfront**: check the daily and weekly screens.
- **Alliance Convoy**: during the morning join window, board a truck; if you're a leader, set a Guardian and escort, and watch the convoy cross the map after it launches.

Please try it on **whatever phone you actually use** — the more different phones and screen sizes, the better.

## What we most want to know

- Anything that looks **broken, cut off, or off-screen** on your phone (a photo helps a lot).
- Anywhere you got **stuck, confused, or couldn't tell what to do next**.
- Buttons that **don't respond**, screens that **freeze or reload**, or text that's **hard to read**.
- Anything that felt **slow**.
- Anything that just felt **unfair or unclear**.
