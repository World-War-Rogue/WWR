# 06. ALLIANCE HIGH COMMAND & 100-MEMBER ROSTER SPECIFICATION

**Folder Category:** Multi-Commander Organizational Hierarchy & Command Structure  
**Security Level:** CLASSIFIED // LEVEL 4 CLEARANCE  
**Max Alliance Size:** Exactly 100 Active Officers  

---

## 1. 100-Member Command Structure

Every alliance operates under a rigid military high-command structure designed to distribute leadership, tactical responsibilities, and territory management:

```
                      [ 1 ADMIRAL / SUPREME COMMANDER ]
                      - Supreme Strategic Authority
                      - Declares Alliance Wars & Pacts
                      - Promotes / Demotes Colonels
                                     |
         +---------------------------+---------------------------+
         |                                                       |
[ 10 COLONELS / TASK FORCE LEADERS ]            [ 89 LIEUTENANTS / COMBAT STAFF ]
- Oversee Sector Garrison Defense                - Field Combat Operations
- Manage Alliance Research Tech Trees            - Resource Scavenging & Raids
- Issue Tactical Strike Directives               - Garrison Reinforcement
- Approve New Member Recruits
```

### Rank Breakdown:
1. **1 Admiral (Supreme Commander):**
   Full administrative authority. Manages alliance name, tag, motto, emblem, diplomatic declarations (Non-Aggression Pacts, Mutual Defense, or Formal War), and appoints Colonels.
2. **10 Colonels (Task Force Commanders):**
   Can declare tactical tasks, execute joint operations, kick disorderly members, and manage sector garrison assignments on the 36-sector map.
3. **89 Lieutenants (Field Officers):**
   Active combatants who contribute combat power, contribute resources to alliance tech research, participate in joint raids, and earn hourly dividends.

---

## 2. Alliance Directives & Joint Tasks

Alliances cooperate on shared milestones refreshed daily and weekly:
- **Daily Scavenge Target:** Aggregate delivery of 500,000 Alloy and 200,000 Fuel to the Alliance Logistics Hub.
- **Weekly Offensive Operation:** Coordinated strike against rogue NPC fortress strongholds.
- **Sector Defense Maintenance:** Keeping minimum garrison defense ratings above 1,200,000 across all held sectors.

---

## 3. High Command Tactical Comms & Announcements

Alliances maintain dedicated internal communications:
- **High Command Sticky Directive:** Prominently displays the Admiral's active strategic order (e.g., *"FOCUS DEFENSE ON SECTOR S-14 OIL REFINERY"*).
- **Officer Channel (Admiral + 10 Colonels):** Encrypted private chat for leadership coordination and war planning.
- **All-Hands Channel:** General discussion for all 100 alliance members.
- **Diplomatic Inquiries:** Channel for incoming treaties, ceasefires, and non-aggression pact proposals from rival alliances.
