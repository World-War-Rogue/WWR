# 04. BASE BUILDING & FORTIFICATIONS SPECIFICATION

**Folder Category:** Engineering & Forward Operating Base (FOB) Architecture  
**Security Level:** CLASSIFIED // LEVEL 3 CLEARANCE  
**System Type:** 2D Isometric Defense Grid & Structural Degradation  

---

## 1. Forward Operating Base Layout

Commanders construct and fortify their FOB using an extensible modular grid. Each structure serves an active defensive or operational function during incoming enemy raids and nighttime survival swarms:

### Core Structures
1. **Tactical Headquarters (HQ):**
   The nerve center of the base. Dictates max building levels, command radar range, squad dispatch limits, and technological tier upgrades.
2. **Phalanx CIWS (Close-In Weapon System):**
   Rapid-fire 20mm rotary Gatling cannon designed to intercept incoming ballistic mortar shells, artillery rounds, and cruise missiles within a 450px perimeter.
3. **155mm Heavy Howitzer Emplacement:**
   Long-range ballistic ordnance providing counter-battery fire against enemy siege squads. Consumes munitions per salvo.
4. **Surface-to-Air Missile (SAM) Silo:**
   Guards FOB airspace against incoming attack helicopters and airborne gunship sorties.
5. **Radar & Electronic Sensor Array:**
   Pierces tactical fog of war, reveals concealed enemy sniper positions, and flags early warning vectors for survival swarms.
6. **Alloy Refinery & Fuel Silos:**
   Stores and processes refined strategic materials. High priority target for attacking raiders; fuel tanks trigger explosive chain reactions if breached.

---

## 2. Fortifications & Barrier Classes

Commanders build physical barrier layers to redirect enemy armor into pre-sighted kill zones:

- **Sandbag Berms:** Low-cost infantry cover; stops 7.62mm and .50 BMG rifle fire.
- **Reinforced Concrete Blast Walls:** Multi-layered barriers designed to deflect shaped-charge HEAT warheads and absorb kinetic direct-fire rounds.
- **Steel Dragon's Teeth:** Anti-tank obstacle rows preventing heavy armor traversal without breaching engineers or carpet bombardment.
- **Directional Claymore & Minefields:** Concealed area-denial devices triggered by vehicle tracks or hostile infantry boots.

---

## 3. Structural Degradation & Repair Cycles

- **Persistent Damage Tracking:**
  Structures do not instantly reset after battles. Hitpoint values and visual degradation (smoke plumes, concrete fractures, fire emitters) persist until engineering squads conduct repairs using refined **Alloy**.
- **Field Engineering Drones:**
  Automated drones stationed in maintenance bays actively patch minor shrapnel damage between waves, provided the base maintains sufficient Alloy stockpiles.
