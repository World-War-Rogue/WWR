## 1. Composite Dominance Scoring Formula

Rankings on the global and server leaderboards are calculated using a balanced composite formula that rewards total combat capability, event dedication, and physical territorial holdings:

$$\text{Composite Dominance Score} = (0.40 \times \text{Combat Power Score}) + (0.35 \times \text{Season Event Score}) + (0.25 \times \text{Territorial Control Score})$$

### Component Formulations:
1. **Combat Power Score (40% Weight):**
   $$\text{Combat Power Score} = \min\left(100, \frac{\text{Alliance Total Power}}{50,000,000} \times 100\right)$$
   Aggregates the total combat rating of all 100 officers in the battlegroup.
2. **Season Event Score (35% Weight):**
   $$\text{Season Event Score} = \min\left(100, \frac{\text{Seasonal Operation Points}}{300,000} \times 100\right)$$
   Reflects milestone completions in *Season 01: Sandstorm Apex*, clearing rogue boss raids and completing campaign sorties.
3. **Territorial Control Score (25% Weight):**
   $$\text{Territorial Control Score} = \min\left(100, \left(\frac{\text{Sectors Held}}{36} \times 70\right) + \left(\frac{\text{Average Garrison Defense Rating}}{2,000,000} \times 30\right)\right)$$
   Rewards both the breadth of territory controlled and the defensive fortification depth of those sectors.

## 3. Hourly Alliance Dividends

Holding strategic territory distributes automated resource dividends directly into the personal stockpiles of all 100 active members every 60 minutes. Alliances holding 6+ sectors gain compounded logistics synergy bonuses.
