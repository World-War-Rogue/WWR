## 1. The Premise: World War Rogue

In the mid-2020s, catastrophic satellite grid failures and the sudden depletion of key rare-earth refining facilities triggered the collapse of conventional centralized international treaties. Regional superpowers fractured into military battlegroups, autonomous expeditionary task forces, and corporate security armadas competing for control over the remaining strategic refining basins, deep-sea trenches, and orbital uplink arrays.

Commanders operate **Forward Operating Bases (FOBs)** on contested frontiers, conducting high-stakes survival resource sweeps, defensive ballistic perimeter fortifications, and coordinated 100-member alliance campaigns.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of Gameplay

1. **Deterministic Ballistic Combat**:
   No invisible dice rolls. Every cannon shell, mortar round, and anti-tank missile has mass, velocity, arc, and kinetic penetration values.
2. **Destructible Tactical Battlefield**:
   Obstacles, sandbag redoubts, reinforced concrete walls, and watchtowers take physical damage from explosive shells and collapse into protective rubble.
3. **100+ Global Military Arsenal Across 4 Eras**:
   Authentic hardware from USA, Germany, UK, France, Japan, Israel, South Korea, Sweden, Canada, Australia, and Ukraine.
4. **Strict 5-Squad Command Discipline**:
   Commanders are limited to 5 active squads (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo), each fielding up to 6 units (maximum 30 units deployed from 100+ roster).
5. **Anti-Predatory Fair Armory**:
   No predatory $99 loot boxes or paywalls. Micro-investments range from $0.99 to $4.99 with guaranteed high utility and generous free tactical play.
6. **100-Member Alliance High Command**:
   1 Supreme Admiral, 10 Colonels, and 89 Staff Lieutenants coordinate across 36 theater sectors with real-time tactical tasks and multi-language auto-translated comms.

## 3. The 3 Campaign Theaters & Seasons

### Season 01: Sandstorm Perimeter (Current Active Season)
- **Terrain:** Arid desert dunes, oil pipeline corridors, and sandstone canyons.
- **Environmental Hazard:** Dust storms impair long-range thermal optics and increase engine fuel consumption by 15%.
- **Ballistics Effect:** High ambient heat causes propellant burns with +5% muzzle velocity, but gusting crosswinds add minor lateral shell drift.

### Season 02: Frostbite Tundra (Upcoming)
- **Terrain:** Glacial fjords, permafrost mountain passes, and frozen naval ports.
- **Environmental Hazard:** Extreme sub-zero temperatures cause hydraulic freezing in uninsulated vehicles and higher rations consumption.
- **Ballistics Effect:** Cold dense air reduces effective projectile range by 8%, but increases radar detection clarity.

### Season 03: Iron Jungle (Upcoming)
- **Terrain:** Dense equatorial rainforest, river deltas, and flooded ruins.
- **Environmental Hazard:** Torrential monsoon rainfall disrupts air support flight paths and grounds lightweight quadcopter drones.
- **Ballistics Effect:** High foliage density causes early detonation of fragile proximity fuses.
